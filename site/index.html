How it works

Everything on this page is calculated in your browser. It loads every Premier League, Championship and League One result since 2014/15 (from football-data.co.uk) and the remaining fixtures (from fixturedownload.com), then fits a Dixon-Coles model. Each team gets an attack and a defence rating on one scale across the three divisions, so clubs just relegated or promoted are rated from their real results. Recent matches count more than old ones.

The page then plays out the rest of the season thousands of times. Each simulated season first draws how good every team “really” is from the uncertainty in its rating, so the odds are not overconfident. The top two go up; 3rd–6th play two-legged semi-finals and a final at a neutral ground; the bottom three go down. Ties in the table are separated by goal difference, then goals scored.
